For the results of my MITOU IT Program FY2025 project, I was certified as a Super Creator by the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and the Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan (IPA).

The project was "Development of a High-Performance, Fault-Tolerant MySQL". As project lead, I developed Kamo, a high-performance, fault-tolerant MySQL-compatible DBMS. The project manager was Kunihiro Tanaka.
